# Every Subscription has exactly one Holder

A Subscription is always held by one Member: no shared or empty Holder, and no cost splitting between Members. Contracts are in one person's name even when the whole family uses the service, and a single Holder removes any doubt about who manages or cancels it. It also keeps "cost by Member", Member Budgets and Reminder routing unambiguous. The price is that a genuinely split bill needs one Holder chosen, or two Subscriptions.
