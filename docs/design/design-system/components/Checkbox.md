# Checkbox

Checkboxes, radio buttons and the switch for choices. Utrecht Checkbox (`utrecht-checkbox utrecht-checkbox--custom`), Radio button and Form toggle.

- **The consumer provides:** the label text and the state; a group of radio buttons sits in a Utrecht Form fieldset with a legend.
- **Rules:** the label is the hit area, at least `target-min` high; a switch (`utrecht-form-toggle`) only for settings that apply at once, such as turning a reminder channel on; checkboxes for choices saved with a button.
