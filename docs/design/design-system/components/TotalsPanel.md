# TotalsPanel

The totals panel heads the dashboard: this month's total, the yearly total and budget use. Abonemen component (`mc-totals`, `mc-meter`).

- **The consumer provides:** totals in the main currency, budget and amount used, and how many items were left out for missing rates.
- **Rules:** totals in `amount-large`; the meter has a text equivalent; over budget is also said in words.
