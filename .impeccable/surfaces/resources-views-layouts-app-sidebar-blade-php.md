## Scope

The Abonemen app shell and the screens in it (dashboard placeholder, settings, households), plus the sign-in and welcome screens. Mode: Operate. Visitor: a Household member checking what renews when, briefly, on a phone or at a desk.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A household view that feels like a paper binder with ink: calm paper pages joined to an ink rail. It refuses the category default of a grey app with one blue button, and the finance-dashboard conventions (navy and gold, hero numbers, glossy charts).

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper ground (#f6f4ef) with white surfaces; one ink blue (#2d3b8f), used as the rail field and the accent, periwinkle (#b4bdff) in dark; warm greys; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (slashed zero) at 16px body, 14px smallest; flat 1px warm borders, 10px corners, no shadow except overlays; 44px controls; red, green and amber only for meaning, always with an icon and words.

STORY: A Household member opens the dashboard, understands in seconds what renews next and what needs action, and trusts that nothing leaves the house.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: a 256px ink rail on the left (mark and name, household switcher, Dashboard, Settings, the User at the bottom); the current destination is a tab of paper joined to the page (the signature move). The page: a page header (h1 28px semibold, one line of description, primary action on the right), then surfaces on paper, content capped at 72rem. Mobile: an ink bar with the mark and a 44px menu button, one column below.

FORM: pinned by the user through the grilling (Shell A, paper and ink), which beats the roll's assignment; the roll was run only to record the reference. seed:2e0fcd37a07c965d

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
